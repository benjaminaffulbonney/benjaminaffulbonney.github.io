About me

I am training to teach physics at secondary level with the National Institute of Teaching, working towards a PGCE, and I am currently on school placement at Laisterdyke Leadership Academy, part of Star Academies.

Before teaching, I studied Biomedical Engineering and trained in clinical engineering at Korle-Bu Teaching Hospital. That experience shapes how I teach: I like to show students where physics appears in real equipment and real problems.

How I teach

I have worked as a SEND support worker and in adult social care, so I am used to adapting to each individual. Those roles taught me patience, clear communication and a strong grounding in safeguarding.

My aim is simple: every learner should leave a lesson understanding something they did not understand before, and still remember it weeks later.
